MODEL.NCB is a modified model: assistant-only LoRA adaptation, adapter merge,
six-bit group quantization, and conversion to Bliss Chat's native format.
These modifications were prepared for Mitch Aiet's Bliss Chat project on
September 15, 2026. They are not an official Liquid AI release or endorsement.
The original publisher checkpoint was preserved unchanged.

TOKENIZER.NCT is a native serialization of the publisher's tokenizer,
including vocabulary padding required by the model, with tokenization and
decoding parity checks. See the model card and source documentation.

The full LFM Open License v1.0 is included as MODEL-LICENSE.txt.
Retain that license and these notices when redistributing the model.
